SCIENTIFIC STATUS

A public experiment, not a discovery engine.

lacuna keeps sourced human-curated questions separate from computed measurements. Metric versions 1 and 2 failed their pre-registered benchmark; the active replacement is a no-human prospective protocol with sealed 2026 predictions, awaiting its registered three-release outcome window.
