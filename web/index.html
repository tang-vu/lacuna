OPEN-SOURCE KNOWLEDGE-GAP RESEARCH

Map the edge of what we know.

Most knowledge maps show what humanity has figured out. lacuna is trying to measure where the connections stop.

The knowledge tree is scaffolding. The holes are the product.

SCIENTIFIC STATUS

A public experiment, not a discovery engine.

lacuna keeps sourced human-curated questions separate from computed measurements. Metric versions 1 and 2 failed their pre-registered benchmark; version 3 remains a draft until its source and benchmark gates are complete.

METHOD AND EVIDENCE

Every exported number points back to its query.

The project publishes versioned OpenAlex artifacts, validation reports, exact or bounded co-occurrence evidence, and the negative results of methods that did not work.

KNOWN LIMITS

The map also publishes what it cannot see.

OpenAlex is thin across parts of the humanities and historical record, and does not represent much craft, practitioner, indigenous, oral, or other non-academic knowledge.

Open source. Provenance first. Failed methods stay visible.
