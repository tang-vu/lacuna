# lacuna v0.2.0 — sealed before the outcome

This release does **not** contain a validated knowledge-gap detector. It advances lacuna from an
auditable negative OpenAlex result to a sealed prospective experiment and publishes a separate,
narrowly claimed empirical evidence track.

## What is included

- The unchanged OpenAlex v1/v2 negative result and its 500 traceable exported measurements.
- A complete sealed 2026 PubMed/MeSH T0: 1,334 official transports, 54,267,874,919 compressed
  bytes, 39,994,988 parsed PubMed records, and 31,110 MeSH descriptors.
- An exhaustive score-free universe of 7,310,895 exact-zero maintained-current PubMed/MeSH pairs.
- A separately frozen, unvalidated Adamic–Adar Q48 formula with five baselines and sealed score
  tuples plus total ordering for all 7,310,895 candidates.
- A monthly watcher that accepts only the next sequential official 2027–2029 source identity and
  abstains on missing, skipped, or conflicting evidence.
- A separate score-blind expression experiment over 499,500 gene pairs in pinned TCGA and
  METABRIC breast-tumour cohorts. It measured 1,599 pairs passing every frozen replication gate,
  zero passing pairs in the independently permuted null, and publishes the strongest 100 bounded
  observations.
- The interactive evidence lab, shareable sourced-hole atlas, metric-blind audit surfaces, and
  static provenance-first web application.

## Scientific status

- **Validated knowledge-gap pairs: zero.**
- The OpenAlex computed layer remains `FAIL`; its rows are measurements from a failed method.
- The autonomous prospective track remains `not_ready` with zero readiness contribution until
  complete 2027, 2028, and 2029 outcomes exist and pass the registered evaluation.
- Sealed candidate scores are immutable predictions from an unvalidated method, not discoveries,
  actionable hypotheses, or evidence that human knowledge is absent.
- Evidence-v1 rows are `replicated_computational_observation` records: bounded cohort-level
  rank-expression associations in the two pinned cohorts only. They are not claims of causality,
  mechanism, clinical utility, cell-intrinsic regulation, novelty, generalization, or knowledge
  gaps.
- Historical metric v3 and BioASQ v2 remain archived audit tracks. BioASQ v2 terminated on its
  development gate without held-out execution.

## Reproducibility

The repository validators re-audit the committed small artifacts and their canonical hashes. The
full candidate index, prediction outputs, PubMed baseline, and 499,500-row expression table remain
off-repository on the data volume and are SHA-pinned by committed manifests. A clean clone can
perform structural and committed-artifact audits; byte-level replay requires those pinned local
files and must never be reported as run when they are unavailable.

The release archive should be cited together with the specific benchmark or result artifact used.
No PubMed source corpus or restricted third-party expression transport is redistributed in the
GitHub release.
